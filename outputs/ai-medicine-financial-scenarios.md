## TEM

- 估值判断：premium; execution-sensitive
- 乐观情景：Diagnostics volume, MRD testing and Data & Applications compound above 25%, while integrations deepen the clinical-data network and adjusted EBITDA continues to scale.
- 中性情景：Revenue grows near 20–25%, data licensing remains strong and margins improve gradually, but the premium valuation requires consistent execution.
- 悲观情景：Growth falls below 15%, acquisitions dilute focus, reimbursement or privacy constraints weaken the data flywheel, or stock compensation prevents durable cash generation.
- 下一验证事件：Q4/FY 2026 earnings and Personalis integration update
- 数据状态：current；财务期：Q3 2026

## SDGR

- 估值判断：premium; execution-sensitive

## RXRX

- 估值判断：pipeline optionality; revenue multiple not decision-useful

- 悲观情景：Clinical signals fail in larger studies, partners stop advancing programs and repeated equity financing absorbs platform upside before approval economics arrive.
- 下一验证事件：FDA path for REC-4881, REC-1245 expansion data and additional partner options
