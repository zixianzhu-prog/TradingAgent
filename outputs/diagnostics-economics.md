## NTRA

文件披露的毛利率数据为产品成本占产品收入的比率（非毛利率本身）：Q3 2026为35.4%，前九个月为35.5%，均较上年同期（35.1%、36.2%）小幅变动，说明成本管控相对稳定。测试量前九个月增长约21%但收入增速达36.5%，主因平均售价提升约13.7%，尤其Signatera（享有ADLT挂牌价保护）占比持续扩大，支付方结构以在网商业保险为主，微缺失筛查覆盖率仍是主要收入压制因素。

- 毛利率 35.4%（three months ended September 30, 2026）：> Overall the cost of product revenues as a percent of total product revenues were 35.4% and 35.1% for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively.
- 毛利率 35.1%（three months ended September 30, 2025）：> Overall the cost of product revenues as a percent of total product revenues were 35.4% and 35.1% for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively.
- 付款方：Signatera获得Medicare Part B覆盖及ADLT地位；初始三个日历季度按挂牌价（实际标价）全额偿付，此后按PAMA私人支付方加权中位数定价。 > CMS has granted Medicare Part B coverage and Advanced Diagnostic Laboratory Test (ADLT) status, which has separate reporting and payment requirements under PAMA, for our Signatera test. New ADLTs are paid at a rate equal to their actual list charge, or publicly available rate, during an initial period of three calendar quarters.
- 付款方：CMS设定的定价基准：非整倍体及微缺失检测802美元，扩展携带者筛查约2,450美元。 > CMS has established a pricing benchmark of $802 for aneuploidy and microdeletions testing, and approximately $2,450 for expanded carrier screening testing.
- 付款方：PAMA报告要求自2021年起暂停，最新延迟至2027年，因此Medicare Part B CLFS费率未出现削减。 > the PAMA reporting requirements were suspended in 2021 and have continued to be delayed, most recently until 2027, which in turn has not resulted in rate reductions under the Medicare Part B CLFS.

## GRAL

GRAL（Grail）的Galleri筛查检测收入在2026年第三季度和前九个月均实现增长，主要由销售量大幅增长（分别同比+24%和+36%）驱动，但平均售价下降（分别-9%和-8%）对增长有所抵消。公司目前尚未获得Medicare或大型商业保险覆盖，报销主要来自自保雇主和TRICARE，标价策略叠加促销折扣使ASP持续承压，合同价及折扣渠道的毛利率低于标价渠道。

- 付款方：Galleri目前已获得多家自保雇主、健康计划及TRICARE的报销覆盖，但尚未获得Medicare或大型商业保险公司的广泛覆盖 > we have established coverage and reimbursement from a number of self-insured employers and health plans, including coverage from TRICARE, but we do not currently have broader coverage and reimbursement by Medicare or large commercial insurers
- 付款方：2026年2月新法律为FDA批准的MCED检测创建了Medicare覆盖福利类别，CMS最早可于2029年1月1日对50-65岁Medicare人群启动覆盖，每年扩展一个年龄段 > new law created a coverage benefit category to enable coverage of FDA-approved MCED tests by Medicare, with authority for Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services ("CMS") to initiate coverage as early as January 1, 2029 for the aged 50-65 Medicare population and expanding one age-year at a time annually
