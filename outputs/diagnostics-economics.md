## TEM

TEM诊断业务在2026年前三季度和第三季度均实现强劲增长，总检测量同比分别增长约20%（季度）和24%（累计），诊断收入亦相应提升。公司高度依赖第三方付款方，肿瘤NGS检测历史付款回收率仅约55%，遗传检测约50%，而xT CDX于2024年中获得ADLT地位有助于改善Medicare报销前景。

- 付款方：临床肿瘤NGS检测付款回收率约55%，遗传检测约50%，基于追溯计量口径（2023年1月至2024年12月完成的检测） > As of December 31, 2025, we had received payment on approximately 55% of our clinical oncology next generation sequencing, or NGS, tests and 50% of our hereditary tests across all payers performed from January 1, 2023 through December 31, 2024.
- 付款方：临床肿瘤检测中Medicare受益人占比26%，适用于2025、2024、2023年 > Approximately 26% of our clinical oncology tests were for Medicare beneficiaries in the years ended December 31, 2025, 2024 and 2023, respectively.
- 付款方：遗传检测中Medicare受益人占比10%，适用于2025年 > Approximately 10% of our hereditary tests were for Medicare beneficiaries in the year ended December 31, 2025.

## CAI

分子分析服务在2026年前三季度收入大幅增长，前九个月同比增幅达49.8%，临床检测量同期从14.67万增至17.35万例，驱动因素为检测量提升和平均售价改善。医保报销方面，MI Cancer Seek定价8,455美元且覆盖率趋于下降（医保占收入比从2025年前九个月46.4%降至2026年39.5%），Caris Assure通过Gapfill定价3,649美元，早期检测产品Caris Detect则仅限自费，Medicare报销尚无计划。

- three months ended September 30, 2026：61,500 次检测，检测收入 $262.7M，每次约 $4,272
- three months ended September 30, 2025：50,800 次检测，检测收入 $207.6M，每次约 $4,087
- nine months ended September 30, 2026：173,500 次检测，检测收入 $725.8M，每次约 $4,183
- nine months ended September 30, 2025：146,700 次检测，检测收入 $484.6M，每次约 $3,303
- 付款方：MI Cancer Seek获NGS NCD国家医保覆盖；MI Tumor Seek Hybrid及Caris Assure（治疗选择）获MolDX计划覆盖 > Our MI Cancer Seek solution has Medicare coverage under the NGS NCD. Our MI Tumor Seek Hybrid solution and Caris Assure solution for therapy selection are covered by Medicare under the MolDX Program.
- 付款方：MI Cancer Seek（CPT 0211U）的CLFS国家定价为8,455美元 > CMS established national pricing at $8,455 under the CLFS.
- 付款方：Caris Assure（CPT 0485U）通过Gapfill方法定价，CLFS价格3,649美元，2026年1月1日起生效 > CMS determined to price Caris Assure using the "Gapfill" method, which resulted in a price under the CLFS of $3,649, effective January 1, 2026.

## NTRA

公司测试量快速增长，九个月内总处理量达315万例（同比+21%），产品收入增长36.5%至22.3亿美元，ASP提升主要由Signatera占比提升及第三方支付方覆盖改善驱动。付款方结构以商业网络内保险为主，Signatera享有ADLT地位并在初始三季度按挂牌价报销；但微缺失筛查覆盖率仍低，多数州Medicaid报销率低或不覆盖，构成持续收入压力。

- 付款方：CMS已为非整倍体和微缺失检测设定802美元的定价基准，扩展携带者筛查约为2,450美元（对应Panorama和Horizon产品线） > CMS has established a pricing benchmark of $802 for aneuploidy and microdeletions testing, and approximately $2,450 for expanded carrier screening testing.
- 付款方：Signatera获得Medicare Part B覆盖及ADLT地位，新ADLT在初始三个日历季度内按实际挂牌价支付 > CMS has granted Medicare Part B coverage and Advanced Diagnostic Laboratory Test (ADLT) status, which has separate reporting and payment requirements under PAMA, for our Signatera test. New ADLTs are paid at a rate equal to their actual list charge, or publicly available rate, during an initial period of three calendar quarters.
- 付款方：PAMA报告要求已暂停至2027年，因此Medicare Part B临床实验室收费表未发生降价 > the PAMA reporting requirements were suspended in 2021 and have continued to be delayed, most recently until 2027, which in turn has not resulted in rate reductions under the Medicare Part B CLFS.

## GRAL

筛查收入在2026年前三季度同比增长24%至1.197亿美元，Q3单季为3730万美元，主要由Galleri销量增长驱动，但促销定价导致平均售价下降。目前公司尚未获得Medicare或大型商业保险的广泛报销，依赖自保雇主等渠道，FDA批准后有望于2029年起启动Medicare MCED类别覆盖；文中未明示具体检测量绝对数值或毛利率百分比。

- 付款方：Medicare是美国最大的单一支付方，对癌症相关检测尤为重要，许多其他支付方以其政策为基准。 > Medicare is the single largest healthcare payor in the United States, and a particularly significant payor for many cancer-related laboratory services given the demographics of the Medicare population
- 付款方：目前Galleri尚未获得Medicare或大型商业保险的广泛覆盖，仅与部分自保雇主及健康计划建立了报销安排。 > we do not currently have broader coverage and reimbursement by Medicare or large commercial insurers
- 付款方：已建立覆盖的支付方包括TRICARE及多家自保雇主和健康计划，尚不包括Medicare或大型商业保险。 > we have established coverage and reimbursement from a number of self-insured employers and health plans, including coverage from TRICARE
