*〔Table under the answer · 答案下方的长期表〕*

**EN**

> **Intuitive** controls robots, instruments and surgeon training; procedures grew 14%. AI is a feature, not the core.
> **Tempus** joins tests, clinical records and molecular data, but Illumina is its only sequencer supplier and a related party supplied about a fifth of its data revenue.

**中文**

> **HeartFlow** 拥有 AI 医疗里最接近"支付瓶颈"的东西：I 类医保收费编码、约 99% 参保人群覆盖、写进心脏病学指南。风险是价格，约为收入的 15 倍。
> **Ceribell** 有首个获 FDA 510(k) 许可、用于诊断癫痫持续状态的 AI 设备，也是唯一获 FDA 许可的谵妄监测设备，并有医保附加支付。亏损仍在扩大。

## 2:05–3:00 · How the list is made · 这份名单怎么来的

*〔Section "How this list is made" · "这份名单怎么来的"五步〕*

**EN**

> Five steps, every day. **One:** catch frontier news. The system has collected more than 2,500 core news articles, plus company filings, FDA device decisions and FDA, CMS and HHS policy records. **Two:** map each change to the companies it affects. **Three:** Serenity reads their filings, using three research skills through eight evidence gates: supply chain, customers, earnings, chokepoint, AI purity, control of several links, counter-thesis and governance. **Four:** check the market. OpenBB supplies prices and valuation, and Kronos gives a five-day forecast. This provides short-term market context, but it does not validate or change the long-term thesis. **Five:** rank, explain and freeze.
>
> Only Serenity-reviewed companies can be picked. Their scores are derived consistently from evidence-gate results and reported financial data, rather than free-form ranking. And potential is shown separately from evidence: a company can have high potential but weak evidence, or strong evidence but limited upside.

**中文**

> 每天五步。**第一**，抓前沿新闻：系统已收集超过 2,500 篇核心新闻，以及公司文件、FDA 器械许可记录和 FDA、CMS、HHS 政策记录。**第二**，把每个变化映射到受影响的公司。**第三**，Serenity 读这些公司的文件，用三个研究 skill 过八道证据门槛：产业链、客户、盈利、瓶颈、AI 纯度、多环节控制、反方论证和治理。**第四**，看市场：OpenBB 提供价格和估值，Kronos 给出 5 天预测。这提供短期市场背景，但不验证、也不改变长期投资逻辑。**第五**，排名、解释、冻结。
>
> 只有经过 Serenity 审查的公司才能入选。分数按固定规则由证据门槛结果和已披露的财务数据推导，而不是自由排序。潜力和证据成熟度也分开显示：一家公司可能潜力高但证据弱，也可能证据扎实但上行空间有限。

**中文**

## Likely questions · 可能被问到的问题

### 1. Why trust Serenity over the news? · 为什么相信 Serenity 而不是新闻？

**EN:** News tells you something happened; it can't tell you whether the company controls anything. Recursion and HeartFlow both had good weeks. Only the filings show that one depends on single-source suppliers and the other holds a billing code competitors don't have.

**中文：** 新闻只能告诉你发生了什么，不能告诉你这家公司是否掌握关键环节。Recursion 和 HeartFlow 这周都有好消息，只有读了文件才知道：一家依赖单一来源供应商，另一家拥有竞争对手没有的医保编码。

### 2. Who does the Serenity analysis? · Serenity 分析是谁做的？

**EN:** An AI reviewer applies the three core Serenity skills through the eight gates: Codex for the first round, Claude for the deeper reviews. Before a review changes any score, every quote must be found word for word in the SEC filing; a quote that doesn't match drops that gate. The current Claude reviews (twelve companies) hold every quote matched word for word. When evidence changes, a review is marked stale until it's redone. The judgement can still be wrong, which is why each review lists what would prove it wrong.

**中文：** 由 AI 审查者用三个核心 Serenity skill、按八道门槛执行：第一轮由 Codex 完成，深度审查由 Claude 完成。任何审查在改变分数之前，每条引用都必须能在 SEC 原文里逐字找到；对不上的引用会让那道门槛降级。目前 12 家公司的 Claude 审查，所有引用都已逐字核对通过。证据一旦更新，旧审查会被标为"已过期"，直到重做。判断本身仍可能出错，所以每份审查都写明了"出现什么情况说明我们错了"。

### 3. Why did Tempus drop from first to third? · Tempus 为什么从第一降到第三？

**EN:** The old ranking used analyst scores. Now the moat, AI-exposure and growth scores come from Serenity's gate results. Tempus's filings show it sits downstream of Illumina, about three quarters of revenue is diagnostics, and a related party supplied about a fifth of data revenue. It is still a pick, just not first.

**中文：** 旧排名用的是人工打分。现在护城河、AI 相关度和增长三项来自 Serenity 的门槛结果。Tempus 的文件显示：它处在 Illumina 的下游，约四分之三收入来自诊断检测，约五分之一数据收入来自关联方。它仍然入选，只是不再排第一。

### 3b. GE HealthCare had a high score. Why isn't it a pick? · GE HealthCare 分数不低，为什么没入选？

**EN:** Before its review it scored 80 on analyst judgement and couldn't be picked. A new FDA clearance triggered its first full Serenity review. The filings show AI is a feature across a large imaging and drug-diagnostics business, so AI purity is only partial and its score fell to 76, below Tempus. The FDA record did add one real point: FDA also cleared a change-control plan that lets GE update the AI model without a new submission.

**中文：** 审查前它靠人工打分得 80 分，但按规则不能入选。一项新的 FDA 许可触发了它的首次完整 Serenity 审查。文件显示，AI 只是一个大型影像和药物诊断业务里的功能，AI 纯度只有 partial，分数降到 76，低于 Tempus。FDA 记录也确实补充了一个实在的优势：FDA 同时许可了一项变更控制计划，GE 以后更新 AI 模型不必重新申报。

### 3c. How fresh is the FDA data? · FDA 数据有多新？

**EN:** It comes from openFDA, which trails FDA decisions by one to two weeks; the latest record today is October 30. A record counts as news from the day we first see it, so old decisions are never replayed as new. Company press releases are next, because they usually announce a clearance the same day.

**中文：** 数据来自 openFDA，它比 FDA 的决定晚 1–2 周，目前最新一条是 10 月 30 日。一条记录从我们第一次看到它那天才算新闻，旧的许可不会被当作新消息。下一步接公司新闻稿，因为公司通常在许可当天就会公告。

### 4. What's still in the queue? · 队列里还有什么？

**EN:** Seventeen reviews need updating because their evidence changed, and two companies have not yet been reviewed. GE HealthCare is next. No company can be picked until it has a current, citation-checked review. The page shows those counts at the top.

**中文：** 有 17 份审查因为证据变化需要更新，另有 2 家公司还没审查。下一个是 GE HealthCare。任何公司在拥有基于当前证据、通过引用核对的审查之前，都不能入选。页面最上方显示了这些数字。

### 5. Does it actually predict stock prices? · 它真的能预测股价吗？

**EN:** Not proven, and I'm not claiming it. The short-term score has been frozen daily since November 2 and backfilled on real trading days. So far it's mixed: at one session the high-score group did worse than the rest, at five sessions it did better, but neither reaches our own bar of 30 observations across 10 prediction days. Serenity tiers were frozen today at Friday's close and will be scored against XBI the same way.

**中文：** 还没有被证明，我也不这么宣称。短期评分从 11 月 2 日起每天冻结，并用真实交易日回填。目前结果好坏参半：1 个交易日后，高分组反而比其他组差；5 个交易日后，高分组更好。但两者都没达到我们自己定的标准（10 个预测日、30 条观察）。Serenity 级别今天按上周五收盘价冻结，之后用同样方法和 XBI 对比。

### 5b. HeartFlow and Recursion both fell. Doesn't that undermine it? · HeartFlow 和 Recursion 都跌了，这不说明有问题吗？

**EN:** HeartFlow fell 19% the day after beating expectations. Serenity's main counter was valuation at about sixteen times sales, and that is the risk that showed up. Recursion's headline said "inflection point", yet it is down 23% in a month; news-first scoring would have ranked it first, and Serenity only gave it starter tier. Serenity judges company quality and names the risks. It does not time the next week.

**中文：** HeartFlow 超预期后第二天跌了 19%。Serenity 写明的最大反方就是估值，约为收入的 16 倍，这次正是这个风险兑现了。Recursion 的新闻标题说"拐点"，一个月却跌了 23%；按新闻优先打分它会排第一，而 Serenity 只给了试探级。Serenity 判断的是公司质量并点明风险，不负责预测下周涨跌。

### 6. Isn't the COVID example cherry-picked? · 新冠案例不是挑出来的吗？

**EN:** We chose it because we know it mattered, so it can't prove an edge alone. That's why the mpox miss is on the page.

**中文：** 我们选它确实是因为事后知道它重要，所以单凭它证明不了优势。这也是页面上同时展示猴痘漏报的原因。

### 7. Can it spot a theme nobody is tracking yet? · 能发现还没人跟踪的新主题吗？

**EN:** A radar watches health news, WHO and preprints with no preset topics. Replayed on history with preprints only, it confirmed the new coronavirus on January 26, 2020, and found Moderna and Vir in filings that day. For mpox in 2022 it stayed silent. Both are on the page.

**中文：** 雷达在不预设主题的情况下监测医疗新闻、WHO 和预印本。只用预印本做历史回放，它在 2020 年 1 月 26 日确认了新冠这个新话题，当天在 SEC 文件里找到了 Moderna 和 Vir；2022 年猴痘时它保持沉默。两个案例都在页面上。

### 8. Does it really read every filing? · 真的读取所有文件吗？

**EN:** No. It monitors new SEC filings from the companies it covers and Federal Register updates from FDA, CMS and HHS that match AI-medicine terms, not every filing in the market.

**中文：** 不是。它监控覆盖公司新提交的 SEC 文件，以及 FDA、CMS、HHS 在 Federal Register 上与 AI 医疗关键词匹配的更新，不是全市场所有文件。

## Delivery tips · 现场提示

- **The answer and "why each one" are the heart of the demo.** That's Peter's question. If time is short, cut the ChatGPT section, never these.
  **答案和"为什么是这六家"是演示的核心**，这才是 Peter 的问题。时间不够就砍"为什么不问 ChatGPT"，不要砍这两段。
- **Say "three core skills, eight gates"**, never "eight skills"; say "Serenity validates", never "Serenity decides".
  **说"三个核心 skill、八道门槛"**，不要说"八个 skill"；说"Serenity 验证"，不要说"Serenity 拍板"。
- **Say "starter tier" and "standard tier", not "buy".** These are research tiers from the skills, not advice.
  **说"试探级""标准级"，不要说"买入"。** 这些是 skill 推导出的研究级别，不是投资建议。
- **Raise the Tempus drop and the HeartFlow/Ceribell additions yourself.** They show the pipeline changes the answer.
  **主动提 Tempus 降位、HeartFlow 和 Ceribell 新进入名单。** 这最能说明这条流程确实改变了答案。
- **End with the question** so Peter helps choose what to analyse next.
  **最后一定要提问**，让 Peter 参与决定下一步分析哪些公司。
